Tactical Modalities & Gear

Maldivian technique guidance for target species and current water clarity

Deep Drop Vertical Jigging Strategy

Focus on steep atoll drop-offs (50m to 120m depths) along the outer channel mouths. Use 150g–250g center-weighted speed jigs or slow-pitch leaf jigs during slack-to-flood tide transitions.

Target Species: Dogtooth Tuna (Woshimas), Greater Amberjack, Rusty Jobfish, Giant Trevally.

Reef Edge Topwater Popping & Stickbaiting

Target white-water breaker zones where outer ocean swells compress against the shallow outer reef flats. Cast 120g–160g wide-cup poppers with 100lb fluorocarbon shock leaders.

Target Species: Giant Trevally (Handhi), Bluefin Trevally, Red Bass, Coral Trout.

Open Ocean Pelagic Trolling Strategy

Troll 2 to 5 miles off the outer atoll barrier along sea temperature breaks and current confluences. Run deep-diver minnows (Rapala X-Rap Mag 30) at 7–9 knots for Wahoo.

Target Species: Yellowfin Tuna (Kanneli), Wahoo (Kurumas), Sailfish (Khibaru), Mahi-Mahi.

Traditional Night Handlining (Rey Masverikan)

Anchor inside the lagoon edge or drift the reef slope at 30m–50m. Use fresh flying fish or scad strip baits with minimum lead weight to maintain a natural drift in the current.

Target Species: Two-Spot Red Snapper (Raiy Mas), Malabar Grouper, Emperor (Rinmas), Green Jobfish.
